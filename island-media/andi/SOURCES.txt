Andi, Dream Ocean · exploration sprites

These WebP images are byte-for-byte copies of the Dream Ocean runtime atlases
from the alhuissi/tactics-rpg-react-template checkout at e06bfdec3. Source paths
are relative to that checkout.

walk.webp
  public/assets/images/spritesheets/playables/andi/walk_8dir/walk_8dir_lpc.webp
  576 × 512. Four cardinal rows match Andi’s original LPC walking cells.
  The diagonals are Dream Ocean artwork based on those cells.

sprint.webp
  public/assets/images/spritesheets/playables/andi/run_8dir.webp
  512 × 512. Use the current production atlas with the original north upper
  body and the corrected north stride. Its source includes editable cells
  and a provenance record under asset-sources/images/spritesheets/playables/andi/run/.

The game describes the base character as a Universal LPC export. The upstream
project and original art sources are listed at:
  https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles

See docs/dream-ocean/art/HERO_SPRITES.md, ART_LICENSES.md and
SPRITE_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md in the Dream Ocean checkout. The audit records
incomplete layer-level credits for Andi’s original LPC sheet and diagonal
derivatives. Their individual attribution and applicable license notices
remain to be resolved for public release.
